01 / Implantology
Top service

Dental implants

Permanent solutions for missing teeth

Full restoration of function and aesthetics with dental implants — the most reliable solution for replacing missing teeth. We use premium implant systems (Nobel Biocare, Straumann) with a lifetime warranty.

What's included:

  • 3D planning — computer modelling to the millimeter
  • Single implants — to replace one tooth
  • Multiple implants — restoring several teeth
  • Full-arch restoration — All-on-4, All-on-6
  • Bone grafting — when there is insufficient bone volume
  • Sinus lift — elevating the sinus floor
98% success rate
Lifetime warranty
Painless
02 / Orthodontics

Orthodontics

Straightening smiles at any age

Correcting bite and aligning teeth for a healthy, beautiful smile. We work with patients of all ages — from children to adults.

What's included:

  • Bracket systems — metal, ceramic, sapphire
  • Clear aligners — invisible treatment (Invisalign)
  • Pediatric orthodontics — early intervention from age 6
  • Lingual braces — fitted to the inside of the teeth
  • Retainers — locking in your result
6–18 months
Invisible options

06 / Hygiene

Professional hygiene

Prevention is the best medicine

Regular professional cleaning is the foundation of healthy teeth and gums. We recommend it every 6 months.

What's included:

  • Fluoride treatment — strengthens enamel
  • Gum treatment — periodontology
