Where to find us

How to reach us

01 / Car

By car

  • From the city center: drive east along Zluky Avenue for about 2 km, the clinic is on your right
  • From the Lviv highway: exit onto Zluky Avenue, then about 1.5 km to the clinic
  • Landmarks: next to the Podolyany shopping center, opposite the gas station
02 / Parking

Parking

  • Free parking next to the clinic (15 spaces)
  • Street parking along Zluky Avenue
  • Podolyany mall parking (100 m from the clinic)
  • Accessible spaces for people with disabilities
03 / Transit

Public transport

  • Trolleybuses: #1, #5, #8 — "Zluky Ave." stop
  • Minibuses: #3, #12, #20, #36 — "Zluky Ave." stop
  • From the stop: 2-minute walk to the clinic
04 / Walking

On foot

  • From Theatre Square: ~15 minutes (1.2 km) along Zluky Avenue
  • From Podolyany mall: 2 minutes (100 m)
  • From the railway station: ~25 minutes (2 km)
Accessibility

Accessible to everyone

Our clinic has a wheelchair ramp and an accessible entrance for people with disabilities. We care about the comfort of every patient.
