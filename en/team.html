03 — Our team

Experts you can trust.

Every one of our doctors is more than a specialist — a professional with years of experience, continuous learning and a genuine love for their craft.

A family of professionals

At Sladent we are more than colleagues — we are a true family. The Prydatko family founded this practice on principles of professionalism, honesty and deep care for every patient. Those values live on in each of us and in every procedure we perform.

Our team combines the experience of multiple generations of dentists with the most modern technology and international standards of care.

Lead dentist

Dr. Yaroslav Prydatko

Lead dentist, clinic founder

15+ years of practice

Dr. Yaroslav Prydatko is the founder and soul of Prydatko Family Dentistry, which has evolved into the modern Sladent. With more than 15 years of experience, he is a recognised expert in comprehensive smile reconstruction and aesthetic dentistry.

His philosophy is simple: every patient deserves the highest level of care, attention and professionalism. It is this dedication that turned a small family practice into one of the most trusted dental clinics in Ternopil.

Specialisations:

  • Aesthetic dentistry and smile design
  • Prosthetics and full-mouth reconstruction
  • Veneers and ceramic restorations
  • Diagnostics and treatment planning

Education and certifications:

  • Ternopil National Medical University
  • International Academy of Esthetic Dentistry (IAED)
  • Digital smile design certification
  • Continuing education at leading European centers
Implantology specialist

Dr. Yurii Prydatko

Implant surgeon

12+ years of practice

Dr. Yurii Prydatko is our lead specialist in dental implantology and oral surgery. His deep knowledge of anatomy, precise technique and use of 3D technology let him achieve exceptional results in even the most complex clinical cases.

Yurii is a master of personalised treatment. Every implant case is planned around the patient's unique anatomy, delivering not only function but natural aesthetics.

Specialisations:

  • Dental implantology (single and multiple implants)
  • 3D planning of surgical procedures
  • Bone grafting and sinus lift
  • Immediate implantation after extraction

Education and certifications:

  • Ternopil National Medical University
  • Specialisation in oral surgery
  • International implantology courses (Switzerland, Germany)
  • Certified for Nobel Biocare and Straumann implant systems
Pediatric dentist

Dr. Svitlana Prydatko

Pediatric dentist, orthodontist

10+ years of practice

Dr. Svitlana Prydatko is our specialist in pediatric dentistry and orthodontics. She has a unique gift: creating an atmosphere where even the most anxious children feel safe and comfortable.

Svitlana believes that a positive first experience at the dentist sets the foundation for a healthy smile for life. So every visit with her is more than treatment — it is a warm, patient and understanding encounter.

Specialisations:

  • Pediatric dentistry (from age 1)
  • Cavity prevention and fluoride treatment
  • Early orthodontic intervention
  • Treatment of baby and permanent teeth

Education and certifications:

  • Ternopil National Medical University
  • Specialisation in pediatric dentistry
  • Child psychology and communication courses
  • Pediatric orthodontics certification
