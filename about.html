Our journey: A family legacy, a modern practice

In the heart of Ternopil, more than fifteen years ago, Prydatko Family Dentistry was born — not just a clinic, but a true family calling, founded on a deep respect for the craft of dentistry and an unwavering dedication to our patients.

The name Prydatko has become synonymous with trust, professionalism and care in our city. Generation after generation, families have come to us knowing they will receive not only high-quality treatment, but also genuine care, compassion and understanding.

"Every smile we treat is more than teeth. It is confidence, comfort and quality of life. That is our responsibility and our pride."
— The Prydatko Family philosophy

Over time, the practice evolved. We invested in the latest technologies — 3D scanning, digital implantology, computer-aided smile design. We trained at the world's leading centers. We grew the team with some of the best specialists in Ukraine.

It was time for a new name that reflected our evolution while preserving the soul of our family.

Sladent is our modern face. A bright, dynamic name for a new era of dental excellence. But beneath the modern facade beats the very same heart — Prydatko Family Dentistry.
